## What should you look for in an IPTV subscription in Canada?

The basic idea is pretty simple. IPTV delivers television over your internet connection, so there is no need for a satellite dish or traditional cable box. TiviLive says its service works through supported IPTV players and devices, including Smart TVs, Amazon Fire TV/Firestick, Android TV, phones, tablets, MAG devices, Windows, Mac, Kodi and VLC.

Internet quality still matters, though. TiviLive recommends at least **16 Mbps for HD** and **25+ Mbps for 4K/UHD**, while also noting that a wired Ethernet connection can provide a more stable experience than weaker Wi-Fi.

That makes the usual shopping checklist fairly practical:

* How many simultaneous screens do you need?
* How long do you want to subscribe?
* Does the service support your device?
* Are the channels and VOD content you actually watch available?
* Can you test the service before committing to a longer plan?

## Is an IPTV subscription legal in Canada?

There is an important distinction here: IPTV is a **technology for delivering television over the internet**. The technology itself does not automatically tell you whether a particular service is authorized to distribute every piece of content it offers.

That is especially relevant when a provider advertises a very large international channel catalogue. TiviLive's own terms describe the company as a reseller and state that content availability can change.

So when comparing an IPTV subscription in Canada, it makes sense to look beyond the headline number of channels and check the provider's current service terms as well.
